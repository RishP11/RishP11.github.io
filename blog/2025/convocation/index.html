Convocation

Created on July 19, 2025

2025

A collection of pictures from the 6th convocation at Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad. The convocation took place on 19th of July, 2025. I received my B.Tech (Bachelor of Technology) degree in Electrical Engineering. Additionally, I was awarded the President of India Gold Medal for outstanding academic performance.

Note: Please feel free to view the images of this event. If you wish to use these images for any purpose, and I do not know you, I request that you contact me and IIT Dharwad admin to obtain the necessary permission(s). Some of these images may be privately owned.

Images are zoomable. Simply click on them to view the high-res versions.

Me, being awarded the President of India Gold Medal. In this image: (from left to right) Chairperson/ Senior Member (BoG, IIT Dharwad), Me, Prof. Abhay Karandikar (Chief Guest - Secretary to the Government of India, Department of Science & Technology, Ministry of Science & Technology, Government of India. On Lien from Professor, IIT Bombay), Prof. Venkappayya R. Desai (Director, IIT Dharwad), and Prof. Dhirendra Katti (Guest of Honor - Director, IIT Goa).
Receiving the degree. In this image: (in the front of the frame; from left to right) Prof. Venkappayya R. Desai (Director, IIT Dharwad), Me, (in the background of the frame; from left to right) Prof. Abhay Karandikar, and Dr Kalyan Kumar Bhattacharjee (Registrar, IIT Dharwad).

Having received the award, I had the honor of administering the pledge/oath.

Taking and administering the oath to all the graduating students.
